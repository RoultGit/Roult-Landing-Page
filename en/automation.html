Who it's for

Is this service a fit?

Before you write to us, a sense of whether this is right for you right now.

It's a fit if

  • Your team repeats the same manual task every week (quoting, reporting, reconciling)
  • You already use digital tools (WhatsApp, spreadsheets, an ERP) but they're not connected to each other
  • Human error in a repetitive task has already cost you time or money

It may not be the time if

  • The task's volume is so low that automating it costs more than doing it by hand
  • The process still changes week to week and hasn't settled down yet
Questions

About automation.

Do I need to switch tools to automate?

No. We connect the tools you already use (WhatsApp, email, spreadsheets, your ERP); the automation works between them, it doesn't replace them.

What if the process changes after it's automated?

The flow gets adjusted; that's why we document how it works, so your team understands what to touch. Minor adjustments are covered if you take monthly support.

How long until I see results?

It depends on scope, but since these are focused workflows rather than a full system, they usually take less time than a custom software project. The exact timeline is set in the written proposal.
