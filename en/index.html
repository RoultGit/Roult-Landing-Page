We've got answers

Is the code in my name?

Yes, always. The repository, infrastructure and credentials are registered to your company. If you want to work with another team tomorrow, you take everything with you.

How much does it cost?

It depends on scope: we give you a fixed price in writing after the first conversation. Each product in the suite has its own monthly price.

What if I want to change something later?

Two rounds of revisions are included during the project. After that, small changes are covered by monthly support or quoted separately.

We already use Excel and WhatsApp. Do we need our own software?

With a small team it can be enough. The problem shows up as you grow: information gets scattered and there's no single source of truth.

What about support after launch?

You can keep just the code, or sign up for monthly support covering monitoring, fixes and small improvements. It's your call.

Where are you based?

We're a remote team across Latin America and the United States. We work over video calls and in writing, in your time zone.
