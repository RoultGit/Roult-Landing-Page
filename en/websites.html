Before you write to us, a sense of whether this is right for you right now.

It's a fit if

  • You don't have a site, or your current one no longer represents what you sell

Questions

About websites.

Do the domain and hosting stay under my name?

Yes. Just like with custom software, the domain, hosting and access are registered under your company from day one.

Can I request changes after delivery?

Yes, we include two rounds of adjustments during the project. After delivery, content changes are made by your own team from the panel; design or structural changes are quoted separately or covered if you take monthly support.

How many pages are included?

It depends on the scope we define together: from a single-page landing to an institutional site with several sections. The exact number of pages is set in the written proposal.
